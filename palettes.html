lines-fall — palette pool

A curated pool of beautiful 3-color flat palettes. Each palette is its own little print, tagged with mood traits (warm/cool, bright/dark, muted/saturated, day/night, rainy/stormy). Time of day and weather don't pin a slot — they form a mood query, and the picker draws a seeded-random palette from the matching subset. Same conditions on the same day always render the same image.
